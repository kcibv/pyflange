    Nomenclature¶

    PyFlange names follow the symbols of IEC 61400-6 Annex G (ref. [2]) where possible. This page maps the parameters and properties of the flange segment classes to the symbols of the standard.

    Geometry¶

    The drawings are schematic: proportions are not to scale, and the fillet radius r, washers and gap are not shown. Z is the shell force acting on the segment, positive in tension.

    Flange segment parameters¶

    PyFlange IEC 61400-6 Unit Description
    a a m L-flange: distance from the inner face of the flange to the bolt axis. T-flange: distance from the flange edge to the bolt axis.
    b b m Distance from the bolt axis to the center line of the shell.
    s s m Shell (tower wall) thickness.
    s_ratio – – Thickness of the lower shell divided by s. The mean thickness is used where the standard requires it.
    t t m Flange thickness.
    R R, D/2 m Outer radius of the shell.
    central_angle – rad Angle of the segment: 2π / nbolts (L-flange) or 2π / npairs (T-flange). The segment width at the shell mid-surface is c = central_angle · (R − s/2).
    r r m Fillet radius between flange and shell.
    Zg Z1, Zdw N Shell force of the segment at rest (dead weight); negative for compression.
    Fv Fp,C (before losses) N Bolt preload before settlement and plastic losses.
    PPL ΔFpl / Fp – Plastic preload loss, as a fraction of Fv (ref. [2], eq. (24)).
    DFT DFTsbw m Coating thickness under the washers, for the settlement loss (ref. [2], eq. (28)).
    settlement_factor – – 1.0, or 0.5 for tension tightening with re-tightening (ref. [2], 6.7.5.1.2).
    Do dB m Bolt hole diameter.
    bolt, nut, washer – – Bolt, nut and washer objects (pyflange.bolts). The washer outer diameter, or the nut bearing diameter without washer, is Dw.
    gap.height k, kdesign m Gap height.
    gap.angle αgap rad Gap angle. The gap length is Lgap = R · αgap.
    gap.shape_factor – – Scales the bolt force curve for gaps that are not sinusoidal (1.0 = sinusoidal).
    tilt_angle αincl rad Inclination of the flange contact surface (ref. [2], G.2.4).
    E, G E, G Pa Elastic and shear modulus of the flange. Poisson's ratio is derived as ν = E/(2G) − 1.
    k_shell kshell,ini N/m/m Initial shell stiffness; if None, eq. G.43 is used.

    Results and intermediate quantities¶

    PyFlange IEC 61400-6 Description
    bolt_axial_force(Z) FS(Z) Bolt axial force.
    bolt_bending_moment(Z) MS(Z) Bolt bending moment.
    bolt_preload F'p,C Preload after losses (ref. [2], eq. (23) without the COV term).
    shell_force_at_rest, bolt_force_at_rest Z1, FS,1 Point 1.
    shell_force_at_tensile_ULS, bolt_force_at_tensile_ULS Z2, FS,2 Point 2.
    shell_force_at_small_displacement, bolt_force_at_small_displacement Z3, FS,3 Point 3.
    shell_force_at_closed_gap Zclose Point 4: shell force that closes the gap.
    bolt_moment_at_rest, ..._at_tensile_ULS, ..._at_small_displacement MS,1, MS,2, MS,3 Bolt moments at the points.
    failure_mode(...) Fu,A, Fu,B, Fu,D, Fu,E Ultimate shell forces of the failure modes (ref. [2], G.1.2).

    The JSON result files (pyflange determ) also report internal quantities, such as a_prime (a'), Z0 (Z0), DZ_gap (ΔZgap), DZ_gap_inclination, DZ_gap_tot, stiffness_correction_factor (αk), polynomial_initial_slope (χini,mod), gap_k_fac (kfac), gap_k_shell (kshell,ini), gap_k_fl (kfl), gap_stiffness_correction_factor (ftot), a_star (a*) and I_tg (Itg).

    Fatigue¶

    PyFlange IEC 61400-6 Description
    MarkovMatrix.range, .mean, .cycles ΔM, Mmean, n Load bins.
    BoltFatigueCurve(diameter, DS_ref, gamma_M) d, ΔσC, γM Bolt S-N curve (ref. [2], 6.7.5.4).
    BoltFatigueAnalysis.damage D Miner's sum.
    BoltFatigueAnalysis.allowable_damage Dallow Allowable damage (1.0 in ref. [2]).
    SMF SMF Stress multiplication factor (model uncertainty, ref. [5], 6.5).
